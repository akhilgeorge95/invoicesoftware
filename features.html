The same invoice, two workflows

One is a spreadsheet, a calculator and a lot of chasing. The other is three clicks. Watch them run side by side.

The manual way

Spreadsheet · calculator · PDF · inbox

11 steps
  1. Open last month's spreadsheet and save a copy
  2. Copy the client's name, address and TRN across
  3. Type every line item and rate by hand
  4. Work out 5% VAT on a calculator Rounding error
  5. Check the totals. Check them again.
  6. Fix the layout, then export to PDF
  7. Write the covering email from scratch
  8. Attach the PDF and send Forgot the attachment
  9. Put a reminder in the calendar to follow up
  10. Chase the payment. Twice. 21 days overdue
  11. Match the bank transfer to the invoice by hand
Paid eventually — after three reminders
VS

With InvoiceSoftware.ae

One place · everything carried over

4 steps
  1. Pick the client — details and TRN are already saved
  2. Pull in tracked time and billable expenses
  3. VAT, totals and the tax-invoice fields fill themselves
  4. Send. Reminders, payment and reconciliation run themselves.
