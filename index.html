UAE compliance

Ready for the FTA e-invoicing mandate

The UAE is moving to a Peppol-based e-invoicing model. Invoices will need to be issued as structured XML in the PINT AE format and exchanged through an Accredited Service Provider. We are building for that model now, so the switch is a setting — not a migration.

  • PINT AE structured XML output
  • Peppol network exchange via an accredited provider
  • Validation before send, with clear error reporting
  • Archived, tamper-evident document storage
Read the compliance guide
Published FTA timeline
1 July 2026

Pilot phase opens

Invited businesses begin exchanging e-invoices, with voluntary adoption open to everyone else.

30 October 2026

Large businesses appoint an ASP

Businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more must have an Accredited Service Provider in place.

1 January 2027

Phase 1 goes live

E-invoicing becomes mandatory for businesses above the AED 50 million revenue threshold.

1 July 2027

Phase 2 goes live

All remaining in-scope businesses must issue e-invoices, having appointed an ASP by 31 March 2027.
